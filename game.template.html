How to play

Each day you get one real, actively-exploited vulnerability from the CISA KEV catalog. Guess its CVSS v3.1 base score in 6 tries.

  • After each guess you're told whether the real score is higher or lower.
  • Colour shows how close you are: exact, hot (≤0.5), warm (≤1.5), cold.
  • Every wrong guess reveals one metric from the real CVSS vector — attack vector, complexity, privileges required, and so on.
  • Use the revealed metrics to actually calculate the score. That's the skill.

A new vulnerability unlocks at local midnight. Everyone gets the same one.
