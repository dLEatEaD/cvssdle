How to play

Each day you get one real, actively-exploited vulnerability from the CISA KEV catalog. Everyone gets the same one, and a new one unlocks at local midnight.

There are two ways to play it. Pick one with the toggle at the top.

Guess the score

Six tries at the CVSS v3.1 base score.

  • After each guess you're told whether the real score is higher or lower.
  • Colour shows how close you are: exact, hot (≤0.5), warm (≤1.5), cold.
  • Every wrong guess reveals one metric from the real CVSS vector — attack vector, complexity, privileges required, and so on.
  • Run out of guesses within half a point and it's recorded as a near miss rather than a flat loss. It isn't a win, but it isn't nothing either.

Build the vector

Four tries to pick all eight metrics. The score is derived from your choice.

  • No metrics are revealed — they're the answer.
  • Each attempt tells you which individual metrics you got right, so you can keep the ones that landed.
  • This is the harder mode, and the honest one: the base score has few enough possible values that you can binary-search it without knowing any CVSS at all. Eight metrics have 2,592 combinations and no shortcut — you have to read the advisory.

One mode per puzzle. Your first guess of the day locks the mode in, because finishing either mode shows you the full answer — the score and the vector. The other mode unlocks with tomorrow's puzzle. Practice puzzles are exempt: switch freely there, nothing is recorded.
